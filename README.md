# CrowdLensAI

CrowdLensAI is a decentralized platform built on Polygon, designed to revolutionize data annotation for AI development. Targeting a $1.57B industry (2023) projected to reach $15B-$50B by 2030, it connects companies with raw datasets to a global freelancer workforce. Leveraging Polygon’s fast, low-cost transactions, the platform ensures instant POL payments, transparent data provenance, and a game-changing reputation system that rewards top performers with higher earnings.

## Project Overview

CrowdLensAI empowers freelancers and companies alike. Companies upload datasets, freelancers annotate and validate them, and smart contracts handle trust and payments. A reputation system tracks quality and speed, paying top annotators more (e.g., $0.10 vs. $0.05 per task), while instant POL rewards replace 20-30 day delays. Freelancers can flag tough datasets for higher pay, turning challenges into opportunities. The MVP aims to onboard 10 freelancers and 2 companies, targeting 100 annotations with 90% quality.

## Why Polygon?

Polygon’s low fees and scalability power microtransactions, escrow, and reputation tracking, making it ideal for this AI-Web3 use case. The platform bridges a growing market to blockchain, driving POL adoption.

## Goals

- Launch an MVP for dataset uploads, annotation, and instant POL payments.
- Prove the concept with a pilot, building a skilled annotator community.
- Expand Polygon’s ecosystem with a scalable AI solution.

## Milestones

- Month 1: Design core systems.
- Month 2: Build and test on Polygon testnet.
- Month 3: Launch on mainnet with pilot users.

## Impact

CrowdLensAI targets niche AI needs with diverse datasets, aiming for 45,000 POL transactions in 90 days post-pilot and 50+ AI firms in year one. Future extensions may include advanced tokenomics and broader data types.
