# CrowdLensAI

CrowdLensAI is a decentralized platform built on Solana, designed to revolutionize data annotation for AI development. Targeting a $1.57B industry (2023) projected to reach $15B-$50B by 2030, it connects companies with raw datasets to a global freelancer workforce. Leveraging Solana’s high-speed, low-cost transactions, the platform ensures instant SOL payments, transparent data provenance, and a game-changing reputation system that rewards top performers with higher earnings.

## Project Overview

CrowdLensAI empowers freelancers and companies alike. Companies upload datasets, freelancers annotate and validate them, and smart contracts handle trust and payments. A reputation system tracks quality and speed, paying top annotators more (e.g., $0.10 vs. $0.05 per task), while instant SOL rewards replace 20-30 day delays. Freelancers can flag tough datasets for higher pay, turning challenges into opportunities. The MVP aims to onboard 10 freelancers and 2 companies, targeting 100 annotations with 90% quality.

## Why Solana?

Solana’s ultra-low fees and high throughput power microtransactions, escrow, and reputation tracking, making it ideal for this AI-Web3 use case. The platform bridges a growing market to blockchain, driving SOL adoption.

## Goals

- Launch an MVP for dataset uploads, annotation, and instant SOL payments.
- Prove the concept with a pilot, building a skilled annotator community.
- Expand Solana’s ecosystem with a scalable AI solution.

## Milestones

- Month 1: Design core systems.
- Month 2: Build and test on Solana devnet.
- Month 3: Launch on mainnet with pilot users.

## Impact

CrowdLensAI targets niche AI needs with diverse datasets, aiming for 45,000 SOL transactions in 90 days post-pilot and 50+ AI firms in year one. Future extensions may include advanced tokenomics and broader data types.
